# Callers returning a missed call you never made

Attendra never calls anyone. It answers the phone. So when several callers in a week say "I had a missed call from this number" or "someone called me from here", those calls did not come from your clinic, and not from the assistant.

## What is going on

Two things cause it, and neither is the clinic's fault:

- **Caller-ID spoofing.** Robocallers and scammers put a real local number on their calls so people pick up. If they picked yours, everyone they rang sees your number and some of them call it back. A new number is picked more often than an old one.
- **An earlier owner.** A number that was someone else's last year still gets calls meant for them.

The Calls page says so when many short calls come from numbers new to you: a caller who was not calling you hears the greeting, says "who is this?" or hangs up within a few seconds.

## What the assistant does

A caller who says they are returning a call hears, once: "This is [your clinic]'s phone assistant. The clinic doesn't usually call out from this line, so the call you got may not have come from us. Are you a client of [your clinic], or can I take a message for the team?" It never asks them for a pet or offers to add one. A client with a request is helped as usual; a message becomes a request for the team that starts "Returned a call:", with the caller's number, so you can check.

## What to do

1. **Report it to your phone provider.** For a number Attendra provides, write to support and say which number and since when: the provider can check whether the number had an earlier owner and report the spoofing to the carriers.
2. **Caller-ID name and call authentication.** Ask the provider to register the clinic's name on the number (CNAM) and to attest the number under SHAKEN/STIR, so carriers mark calls from it as genuine and calls faking it as suspect.
3. **Say nothing more to the callers.** The assistant already tells them the call did not come from here. There is nothing for the team to call back unless the caller left a message.
4. **It passes.** Spoofers rotate numbers; a week or two later the calls stop. If they keep coming for a month, ask the provider for a new number.
