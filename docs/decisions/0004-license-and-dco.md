# 4. AGPL-3.0 and the DCO, without a CLA

**Status:** accepted, 2026-09-28

The core is AGPL-3.0-only. A practice or developer can run and change it freely; anyone who offers a modified version as a network service must share their changes. That keeps improvements to a safety-sensitive system in the open.

Contributions are accepted under the Developer Certificate of Origin: contributors sign off each commit to certify they may submit it under the project's license.

A DCO does **not** give Elegant IT Limited the right to relicense contributors' code. Offering a commercial (non-AGPL) license to the whole codebase would need a Contributor License Agreement or explicit permission from every contributor. We are not adopting a CLA now: it slows first contributions, and the hosted service we plan runs the AGPL code as is. If that changes, it will be proposed here first and would apply only to future contributions.
